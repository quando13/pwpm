# Business Stories

## Purpose

Business Stories describe real investment situations from the customer's perspective.

They are written in business language rather than technical language and serve as evidence for designing the domain model.

PwPM follows an evidence-driven design approach: every core business concept should be justified by one or more real investment stories.

---

# BS-01 — Rental Property Investment

## Background

I own an apartment purchased as an investment.

The property was financed using both my own capital and a bank loan.

The apartment is rented out to generate monthly rental income.

Every month I receive rent, pay loan principal and interest, and occasionally pay maintenance or renovation costs.

Over time, the property's market value changes.

I periodically update the estimated value based on reliable market references.

## Goal

I want to understand the financial health of this investment at any point in time.

I want to know:

* How much capital I have invested.
* How much loan is still outstanding.
* How much cash flow the property generates.
* How much equity I currently own.
* What my actual investment return is.
* What would happen financially if I sold the property today.

## Success

I no longer maintain multiple Excel files.

Every financial event is recorded once.

All reports are generated automatically from the same data.

# BS-02 — Equity Investment

## Background

I invest in publicly listed stocks.

I may purchase the same stock multiple times at different prices.

I sometimes sell part of my holdings while keeping the remaining shares.

Some companies distribute dividends.

The market price changes every trading day.

I periodically update my portfolio valuation.

## Goal

I want to understand the financial health of every stock investment and my overall portfolio.

I want to know:

* My total invested capital.
* Average acquisition price.
* Current market value.
* Unrealized gain or loss.
* Realized gain or loss.
* Dividend income.
* Overall portfolio performance.
* The impact of buying or selling on my portfolio.

## Success

My investment history is preserved.

Performance is calculated consistently.

I can evaluate my portfolio without building spreadsheets.
