# Customer Workflow

## Purpose

This document describes how customers continuously interact with PwPM during the lifecycle of managing their investments.

It defines the core workflow of the MVP and serves as the foundation for product capabilities, business domains and future application design.

The workflow focuses on investment management rather than investment execution.

Customers come to PwPM because they already own investments and need a better way to understand, maintain and evaluate them.

---

# Guiding Principles

The customer should:

* Enter information only once.
* Own all investment data.
* Spend minimal effort maintaining data.
* Always see a consistent financial picture.
* Be able to make investment decisions quickly.

PwPM exists to reduce manual work while increasing financial clarity.

---

# Customer Workflow

```
Register
      ↓
Maintain
      ↓
Evaluate
      ↓
Decide
```

This workflow repeats continuously throughout the lifecycle of every investment.

---

# Step 1 — Register

## Goal

Digitize an investment for the first time.

Customers already own an investment before entering PwPM.

PwPM is not responsible for helping customers acquire investments.

Instead, PwPM becomes the digital record of that investment.

Examples:

* Register a rental apartment.
* Register stock holdings.
* Register a business investment.
* Register a fixed deposit.
* Register cryptocurrency holdings.

### Customer Outcome

> "My investment now exists inside PwPM."

---

# Step 2 — Maintain

## Goal

Keep investment information up to date.

Customers continuously record changes affecting their investments.

Examples include:

### Financial Transactions

* Rental income
* Loan repayment
* Interest payment
* Dividend received
* Additional investment
* Partial disposal

### Business Events

* Property valuation
* Tenant replacement
* Interest rate update
* Business valuation
* Legal status update

The customer should only record new information.

PwPM updates every dependent calculation automatically.

### Customer Outcome

> "My portfolio always reflects my latest investment situation."

---

# Step 3 — Evaluate

## Goal

Transform investment data into meaningful information.

PwPM automatically generates:

* Investment performance
* Cash Flow
* Portfolio allocation
* Outstanding financing
* Current valuation
* Investment return
* Historical trends

Customers should never recreate spreadsheets to understand their investments.

### Customer Outcome

> "I understand how every investment is performing."

---

# Step 4 — Decide

## Goal

Support investment decisions.

Customers use PwPM to answer questions such as:

* Should I continue holding?
* Should I sell?
* Should I refinance?
* Should I add more capital?
* Which investment performs best?
* How does this investment affect my overall portfolio?

PwPM provides information, analysis and context.

Investment decisions always remain under customer control.

### Customer Outcome

> "I can make investment decisions confidently."

---

# Workflow Characteristics

The workflow is:

* Continuous
* Data-driven
* Event-driven
* Investment-centric
* Decision-oriented

The same workflow should apply consistently across different investment types.

---

# Product Promise

PwPM promises that customers will:

* Capture information once.
* Reuse information everywhere.
* Maintain ownership of their financial data.
* Always work from a single source of truth.
* Spend less time managing spreadsheets.
* Spend more time making investment decisions.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful customer should be able to:

* Register an investment within minutes.
* Update new transactions or events quickly.
* Instantly understand current investment performance.
* Access consistent reports without manual calculations.
* Make investment decisions based on complete and trusted information.
