# Ubiquitous Language

## Purpose

This document defines the common business language used across PwPM.

Every product owner, business analyst, developer and data engineer should use these definitions consistently.

---

## Investment

A financial commitment of capital with the expectation of generating future financial value.

Examples:

* Rental Property
* Equity
* Gold
* Deposit
* Private Business

---

## Portfolio

The complete collection of investments owned by an investor.

---

## Transaction

A financial activity that changes the financial position or cash flow of an investment.

Examples:

* Purchase
* Sale
* Rental Income
* Dividend
* Interest Payment

---

## Reference Event

A business event that changes investment context without directly changing cash flow.

Examples:

* Property Valuation
* Tenant Replacement
* Stock Split
* Interest Rate Change

---

## Financing

The funding structure used to acquire or maintain an investment.

Examples:

* Personal Capital
* Bank Loan

---

## Valuation

An assessment of an investment's estimated value at a specific point in time.

---

## Cash Flow

Money moving into or out of an investment.

---

## Capital

Money contributed by the investor.

---

## Equity

The investor's ownership value after deducting financing obligations.

---

## Performance

The financial outcome generated by an investment over time.

---

## Portfolio Health

A consolidated view describing the financial condition of the entire investment portfolio.

---

## Source of Truth

The authoritative business data entered and owned by the customer.

All reports and analytics must be generated from this data.
